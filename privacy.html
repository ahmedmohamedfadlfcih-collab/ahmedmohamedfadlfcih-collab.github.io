Privacy policy – Elgeneh Publisher

Elgeneh Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel «الجنيه بيتكلم».

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channel. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people.

The access token is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private repository and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
